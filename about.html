About KOYINTA

A technology-driven supply partner specializing in precision fluidic control, HPLC laboratory safety, and micron-level chemical filtration.

Engineering Coordinates of Absolute Precision & Safety

KOYINTA was founded to bridge the gap between high-demanding analytical laboratories, industrial fluid systems, and the underlying material science of chemical filtration.

Whether in high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) mobile phase delivery, toxic VOC laboratory exhaust recovery, or aggressive industrial solvent filtration, precision fluidics cannot tolerate failure. A single leaky thread, sub-micron particle bypass, or material degradation under organic solvents causes costly instrument downtime and lab safety hazards.

That is why KOYINTA avoids generic commodity trading. We specialize in virgin PTFE, PEEK, and Borosilicate 3.3 glass engineering, delivering leak-free GL45 safety caps, chemically inert capsule filters, and vacuum filtration hardware tailored for global B2B partners and OEMs.

All laboratory safety caps and filtration components are manufactured in compliance with FDA, RoHS, and REACH standards, backed by batch-traceable CoA inspection reports.
