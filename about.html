About KOYINTA

An agile sourcing and supply partner dedicated to precision fluidic control, HPLC laboratory safety caps, and chemical filtration components.

Your Technical Sourcing & Quality Partner in China

KOYINTA was founded as an agile technical sourcing partner in precision fluidics. We bridge international laboratory dealers, equipment maintainers, and industrial OEMs with top-tier precision manufacturing resources in China.

Sourcing fluidic components directly from unverified factories often involves communication hurdles, high minimum order quantities (MOQs), and inconsistent batch quality. KOYINTA solves this by acting as your dedicated local quality gatekeeper.

We specialize in Virgin PTFE, PEEK, and 3.3 Borosilicate glass components, offering 100% pre-shipment inspection, low-MOQ laser branding, and fast international express delivery—giving you complete peace of mind and maximum supply agility.
